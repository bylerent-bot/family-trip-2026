Puerto Rico

Rincón, with Old San Juan at each end

Punta Higuera lighthouse, Rincon. Photo: P. Hughes, CC BY 4.0 (source)

A pool house in Rincón, a surf town you can walk around, with a night in Old San Juan on the way in and on the way out so nobody ends up driving the west side in the dark with Taylor. There are nonstops from Norfolk and Richmond, real winter surf and a boat dive for anyone who wants it, and the pool and the calm side of the beach for the kids.

What's nice about it

  • It's the US, so US hospitals and pharmacies, dollars and phones that just work.
  • JetBlue announced a Sunday nonstop from Norfolk and a Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday nonstop from Richmond. Daniel would connect once from Austin.
  • There's a lot to do right from the house: December surf at Maria's, Domes and Sandy Beach, a two-tank boat dive at Desecheo Island, the Gozalandia waterfall, maybe an Isabela surf day.
  • Old San Juan on foot at each end, with El Morro, the walls and a good dinner.

The catches

  • December is big-wave season, so the kids would probably stick to the pool and the calm-side beaches. Reef snorkeling is more of a summer thing.
  • There aren't many restaurants in town, so most meals would likely be at the house, maybe with a chef for two nights.
  • The six-bedroom house we'd probably ask about first is about a mile from the plaza. The one that's an easy walk to town has five bedrooms.
  • The Norfolk nonstop doesn't fly Saturdays, and its times still need to be confirmed on jetblue.com.
  • Rincón has no hospital. The nearest ER, with a separate one for kids, is in Aguadilla, roughly 25 to 30 minutes away, though we haven't drive-checked that.

Weather that week. Dry-season tropics, with highs in the mid-80s, lows around 70, quick trade-wind showers and the sea about 80 degrees. The west coast gets winter swell.

Anna Maria Island

Holmes Beach, Florida

Tranquil Gulf-side beach with sea oats, turquoise water and white sand, Holmes Beach (Anna Maria Island). Photo: Ann Caron, CC BY-SA 3.0 (source)

A pool house a short walk from the Gulf, on an island where you can bike everywhere and walk the little villages. There are nonstops from all three airports, beach and pool every day for the kids, and Sarasota and St. Petersburg within an hour. No surf though, since the Gulf is usually flat in December.

What's nice about it

  • About six hours door to door. Breeze flies Norfolk to Tampa nonstop, Richmond has had the same, and Austin has nonstops to Tampa.
  • Beach and pool every day for a three-year-old, bikes for a seven-year-old, and Pine Avenue and Bridge Street for the adults.
  • Sarasota and St. Petersburg are 30 to 60 minutes away, with the Ringling, Selby Gardens' holiday lights (2026 dates still to confirm), the Chihuly Collection (picking up from Mom's July trip) and the Mote aquarium.
  • A Crystal River manatee snorkel in peak season, about 2 to 2.5 hours up the coast, a boat day to Egmont Key, and mangrove paddles.

The catches

  • No surf, and the Gulf is usually flat in December, so the more active days would be a manatee morning, a boat day and bikes.
  • Gulf water runs in the mid to upper sixties, so the pool would probably do most of the swimming duty.
  • The main listing has five true bedrooms plus a den with a bunk and trundle, so we'd want to confirm whether there's a real sixth room.
  • The big question this late is holiday-week availability on the island. Listed peak rates for six-bedroom houses run $1,755 to $2,251 a night before taxes and fees (checked Oct 21).
  • There are a few restaurants on the island, but most meals would probably be at the house, maybe with a chef for New Year's Eve.

Weather that week. Highs around 70 to 75, lows in the 50s, Gulf water in the high 60s. Beach and pool weather most days, though a cold front can bring wind.

Park City

Old Town, Utah

Main Street Park City after fresh snow, Town Lift footbridge and mountain beyond, string lights overhead. Photo: Michelle Maria, CC BY 3.0 (source)

An Old Town house with Main Street and the Town Lift right there, so the skiers could ride five or six days from the house and everyone else would have Main Street to walk, plus a hot tub every night. It's cold, it sits near 7,000 feet, and about half of us don't ski.

What's nice about it

  • Park City Mountain from the Town Lift, a walk from the house, for five or six riding days. Canyons Village is on the same pass, and there could be a Big Cottonwood powder day too.
  • Main Street has shops, galleries, restaurants and the free trolley, and there'd be a hot tub at the house every night.
  • Utah Olympic Park and the Comet bobsled for Kristina, who's wanted to bobsled since Lake Placid (season dates still to confirm).
  • The Viking Yurt sleigh-ride dinner, the kind of music-with-dinner evening Mom likes.
  • Daniel would have a nonstop from Austin to Salt Lake.

The catches

  • About half of us don't ski or ride, so the week would lean on the village, the spa, tubing and skating for everyone else.
  • It's high up. Old Town sits near 7,000 feet.
  • It would be a cold week for anyone not skiing, with highs in the 20s and 30s.
  • Peak-week pricing is steep. A six-bedroom Old Town house over New Year's runs $35,000 to $55,000 for the week, before flights and lift tickets.

Weather that week. Cold, dry and sunny, with highs in the 20s and 30s and lows in the teens. Snow depends on the year. December averages 30 to 65 inches depending on the source.

Asheville

Blue Ridge, North Carolina

Biltmore House, front lawn and fountain. Photo: Blake Lewis, CC BY-SA 2.0 (source)

A six-bedroom house with a hot tub fifteen minutes from downtown, about seven hours of driving from Virginia Beach, or eight to nine with stops. Biltmore at Christmas, the Grove Park spa and gingerbread houses, and maybe skiing at Cataloochee as a bonus rather than the plan.

What's nice about it

  • We could drive down, so no airports for nine of the ten of us, and we'd have three family cars for the week.
  • Biltmore's Candlelight Christmas Evening, Olmsted's gardens, and the Grove Park Inn's National Gingerbread House display.
  • Posana downtown does steak, short rib and lobster gnocchi.
  • Hikes and the spa, with a Cataloochee or Sugar Mountain day if the snowmaking holds, and Bent Creek or DuPont for bikes.
  • A fireplace, a hot tub and everyone under one roof, the kind of place Mom likes.

The catches

  • Cataloochee gets about 5 inches of natural snow in December and runs on snowmaking, so a warm spell can close it.
  • Snow in town is unlikely. December runs a 51-degree high and a 31-degree low.
  • The heated pool at Asheville 12 costs extra and isn't confirmed for winter.
  • Daniel would be looking at a connection or a long drive from Austin. A seasonal Allegiant nonstop isn't confirmed for December.

Weather that week. December highs around 51 and lows around 31, with snow in town unlikely. The ski areas run on snowmaking, and a warm week can shut them.

Lake Placid

The Olympic village, upstate New York

Mirror Lake frozen under snow, High Peaks and village shoreline behind. Photo: Chris1093, CC BY-SA 3.0 (source)

The family's February base, again, but this time with a hot tub and the kids along. Main Street, the Mirror Lake loop, the Olympic Center and the skating oval are all walkable, Whiteface is twenty minutes by car, and everyone including Mom skied it last time. Two things to know as of October 21. The bobsled ride is listed as unavailable for track updates, and the walkable six-bedroom houses with hot tubs already show booked for the week.

What's nice about it

  • The village is easy to walk, with Main Street shops and restaurants, the paved 2.7-mile Mirror Lake loop, outdoor skating on the Olympic oval, dog-sled rides on Mirror Lake daily from December 26, the Olympic Museum and the 1980 arena.
  • Whiteface, the mountain all five riders skied in February, has snowmaking on nearly every acre. Mom could do cross-country on the 1932 and 1980 Nordic trails at Mt Van Hoevenberg at the 70-and-up rate.
  • It's a drive instead of a flight day, about 10 hours 40 minutes from Virginia Beach with a night in the Hudson Valley on the way up, the way Patrick did it last time. Daniel would fly to Albany.
  • The Mirror Lake Inn spa, the Wild Center and the skating oval for the kids (Taylor skates free), and a hot tub for Mom.
  • Roughly $28,000 to $36,000 for ten with the one house we've confirmed is open.

The catches

  • The bobsled, the main reason Kristina wanted to come back, can't be booked right now. The venue's page redirects and the tourism site says it's unavailable for track updates. The skeleton ride that is offered is ages 13 and up, so only the adults could go.
  • The walkable six-bedroom houses with hot tubs (Hillcrest Haven, the Stevens house, the Waterfront house) all show booked for December 27 to January 3. The one house confirmed open, Cliff View Loj, is under a mile out, which one reviewer called a 30-minute walk. Camp Kobl Hill could change the picture, but it needs a phone call.
  • It's cold, with highs near 28 degrees and nights near zero.
  • Ten to eleven hours of driving each way, and winter on I-87 and Route 73 can add hours.
  • The toboggan chute needs a foot of lake ice, which probably won't happen before January.

Weather that week. Reliably wintry with short days. December highs are about 28 degrees, nights near zero, and about 104 inches of snow a year. Whiteface has snowmaking across nearly all of its skiable acres.

What about Stowe, Vermont?

Stowe has a house with a hot tub, a real spa at Topnotch or the Trapp Family Lodge, an Epic mountain for all five riders, and the groomed recreation path from the village. The drive is 12 to 13 hours from Virginia Beach, one to two more than Lake Placid. The village is small, so the house would need to be right in it to be walkable, and there's no bobsled. Roughly $26,000 to $42,000 for ten, with two six-bedroom hot-tub houses found and neither priced for the week yet.

Mont-Tremblant

The pedestrian village, Quebec

Pedestrian village colorful roofs in snow with ski runs behind. Photo: Joe Shlabotnik from Forest Hills, Queens, USA, CC BY 2.0 (source)

A car-free village at the base of the mountain, lit up at night, with the skating rink, the Aquaclub water park, the open-air Cabriolet gondola and two dozen restaurants on foot. The mountain is on the low side (about 750 to 2,900 feet) and it's on the Ikon pass. One six-bedroom house with a private hot tub is open and priced for our exact week. Getting there would be a one-connection flight to Montreal for each household, then 90 minutes to two hours of winter driving and a border. Nights run near 5 degrees.

What's nice about it

  • A car-free village with heated walkways, a free lit skating rink with ice slides and free skate loans, the Cabriolet, sleigh rides from the village, and the Brind'O Aquaclub for a cold afternoon.
  • The Scandinave spa is ten minutes away, an outdoor thermal circuit where everyone keeps quiet.
  • Plenty to do besides skiing: dog sledding seven minutes away where kids can ride with the guide from age 3, guided snowmobiles, an ice-climbing intro, and 80 kilometers of Nordic trails at Domaine Saint-Bernard.
  • Tremblant's own advice for the holiday week is to start on the North Side and ride the upper lifts to skip the 10 AM South Side line, and January 1 is usually lighter.
  • Panache 784 is open and priced for December 27 to January 3, with six bedrooms and a private outdoor hot tub, about $27,900 to $31,000 for the week with fees.

The catches

  • Every household would connect once to Montreal, then drive 90 minutes to two hours north in winter, probably after dark on arrival day, with a border each way.
  • The house is a shuttle ride from the village, not a walk. The listing says shuttle or a short drive, one guest calls it a 25-minute uphill hike, and another says it's a five-minute walk from the shuttle stop. This one probably only makes sense if we're fine using the free resort shuttle to get home.
  • Nights near 5 degrees and highs in the 20s, and wind can close the summit lifts.
  • There's no hospital in Mont-Tremblant. The nearest 24-hour ER is about 40 minutes away in Sainte-Agathe, so we'd want to bring epinephrine from home.
  • Roughly $45,000 to $66,000 for ten at the current exchange rate.

Weather that week. Real winter. December highs are about 27 degrees with lows about 14, and January highs are about 19 with lows about 5. Snow is likely. Wind can close the summit lifts and leave the lower half open.

Money, in two currencies

Prices here are in US dollars at 1.42 Canadian dollars to the US dollar, the mid-October rate. The rate moved from 1.40 to 1.42 over the previous month, so the Canadian-priced lines could shift a few percent either way. Vrbo quotes in US dollars, and Airbnb and the local operators quote in Canadian dollars with about 18 percent of tax and resort royalty on activities.

Not in any line: the Kidz Club and Snow School, which don't publish prices and fill up months ahead for this week.

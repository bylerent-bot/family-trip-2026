Puerto Rico

Rincón, with Old San Juan at each end

Punta Higuera lighthouse, Rincon. Photo: P. Hughes, CC BY 4.0 (source)

A pool house in Rincón, a surf town you walk around, with a night in Old San Juan on the way in and the way out so nobody drives the west side in the dark with Taylor. No passports, nonstops from Norfolk and Richmond, US medicine, sea level, real winter surf and a boat dive for the crew, pool and the calm side of the beach for the kids.

Why it works

  • US soil: no passports, US hospitals and pharmacies, dollars, phones that just work.
  • JetBlue announced a Sunday nonstop from Norfolk and a Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday nonstop from Richmond. Daniel connects once from Austin.
  • Real adventure from the door: December surf at Maria's, Domes and Sandy Beach, a two-tank boat dive at Desecheo Island, the Gozalandia waterfall, an Isabela surf day.
  • Old San Juan on foot at each end: El Morro, the walls, a good dinner.

The catch

  • December is big-wave season, so the kids stay on the pool and the calm-side beaches, and reef snorkeling is a summer thing.
  • Gluten-free restaurants are thin. Most meals are at the house, with a chef for two nights.
  • The best six-bedroom house found is about a mile from the plaza. The one that is an easy walk to town has five bedrooms.
  • The Norfolk nonstop does not fly Saturdays, and its clock times still need to be confirmed on jetblue.com.
  • Rincón has no hospital. The nearest ER, with a separate one for kids, is in Aguadilla, about 25 to 30 minutes away by an estimate we still need to drive-check.

Weather that week. Dry-season tropics: highs in the mid-80s, lows around 70, brief trade-wind showers, sea about 80 degrees. Winter swell on the west coast.

Anna Maria Island

Holmes Beach, Florida

Tranquil Gulf-side beach with sea oats, turquoise water and white sand, Holmes Beach (Anna Maria Island). Photo: Ann Caron, CC BY-SA 3.0 (source)

A pool house a short walk from the Gulf on a bike-everywhere island with walkable villages. Nonstop flights from all three airports, beach and pool every day for the kids, and Sarasota and St. Petersburg within an hour. No surf; the Gulf is usually flat in December.

Why it works

  • About six hours door to door. Breeze flies Norfolk to Tampa nonstop, Richmond has had the same, and Austin has nonstops to Tampa.
  • Beach and pool every day for a three-year-old, bikes for a seven-year-old, Pine Avenue and Bridge Street for the adults.
  • Sarasota and St. Petersburg are 30 to 60 minutes away: the Ringling, Selby Gardens' holiday lights (2026 dates to confirm), the Chihuly Collection (Mom's July trip, continued), the Mote aquarium.
  • Crystal River manatee snorkel in peak season, about 2 to 2.5 hours up the coast, a boat day to Egmont Key, mangrove paddles.

The catch

  • No surf, and the Gulf is usually flat in December; the crew's days are a manatee morning, a boat day and bikes.
  • Gulf water runs mid to upper sixties, so the pool carries the swimming.
  • The flagship listing has five true bedrooms plus a den with a bunk and trundle; confirm a real sixth room.
  • Holiday-week availability on the island is the real question this late. Listed peak rates for six-bedroom houses run $1,755 to $2,251 a night before taxes and fees (checked Oct 21).
  • One celiac-aware restaurant on the island (The Waterfront, a single undated review). Most meals at the house, with a chef for New Year's Eve.

Weather that week. Highs around 70 to 75, lows in the 50s, Gulf water in the high 60s. Beach and pool weather most days; a cold front can bring wind.

Park City

Old Town, Utah

Main Street Park City after fresh snow, Town Lift footbridge and mountain beyond, string lights overhead. Photo: Michelle Maria, CC BY 3.0 (source)

An Old Town house with Main Street and the Town Lift at the door: five or six riding days from the house, a walkable Main Street for everyone else, a hot tub every night. It is cold, it sits near 7,000 feet, and half the party does not ski.

Why it works

  • Park City Mountain from the Town Lift, a walk from the house, five or six riding days. Canyons Village on the same pass. A Big Cottonwood powder day for the crew.
  • Main Street: shops, galleries, restaurants, the free trolley, a hot tub at the house every night.
  • Utah Olympic Park and the Comet bobsled for Kristina, who has wanted to bobsled since Lake Placid (season dates to confirm).
  • The Viking Yurt sleigh-ride dinner for Mom: the music-with-dinner evening she writes up.
  • A nonstop for Daniel from Austin to Salt Lake.

The catch

  • Half the party does not ski or ride, so the week leans on the village, the spa, tubing and skating for them.
  • Altitude: Old Town sits near 7,000 feet.
  • A cold week for the relaxed crew, with highs in the 20s and 30s.
  • Peak-week pricing: a six-bedroom Old Town house over New Year's runs $35,000 to $55,000 for the week, before flights and lift tickets.
  • Luna's Kitchen is the one dedicated gluten-free kitchen found in town, counter-service and closed Sundays; the two-kitchen house gives Sara her own kitchen.

Weather that week. Cold, dry, sunny days: highs in the 20s and 30s, lows in the teens. Snow depends on the year; December averages 30 to 65 inches depending on the source.

Asheville

Blue Ridge, North Carolina

Biltmore House, front lawn and fountain. Photo: Blake Lewis, CC BY-SA 2.0 (source)

A six-bedroom house with a hot tub fifteen minutes from downtown, about seven hours of driving from Virginia Beach, eight to nine with stops. Biltmore at Christmas, the Grove Park spa and gingerbread houses, a dedicated gluten-free restaurant that serves real food, and skiing at Cataloochee as a bonus, not a plan.

Why it works

  • Drive down: no airports for nine of the ten, and three family cars for the week.
  • Biltmore's Candlelight Christmas Evening, Olmsted's gardens, the Grove Park Inn's National Gingerbread House display.

  • Cataloochee gets about 5 inches of natural snow in December and runs on snowmaking; a warm spell closes it.

  • The heated pool at the lead house is a paid extra and unconfirmed for winter.
  • Daniel's Austin flight is a connection or a long drive; a seasonal Allegiant nonstop is unconfirmed for December.

Weather that week. December highs around 51, lows around 31, snow in town unlikely. The ski areas run on snowmaking and a warm week can shut them.

Lake Placid

The Olympic village, upstate New York

Mirror Lake frozen under snow, High Peaks and village shoreline behind. Photo: Chris1093, CC BY-SA 3.0 (source)

The family's February base, done again with the two things that were missing: a house with a hot tub and the kids along. Main Street, the Mirror Lake loop, the Olympic Center and the skating oval are all on foot, Whiteface is twenty minutes by car, and everyone including Mom skied it last time. Two things to know as of October 21: the bobsled ride is listed as unavailable for track updates, and the walkable six-bedroom houses with hot tubs already show booked for the week.

Why it works

  • The village passes the walk-out test easily: Main Street shops and restaurants, the paved 2.7-mile Mirror Lake loop, outdoor skating on the Olympic oval, dog-sled rides on Mirror Lake daily from December 26, the Olympic Museum and the 1980 arena.
  • Whiteface, the mountain all five riders skied in February, with snowmaking on nearly every acre; cross-country on the 1932 and 1980 Nordic trails at Mt Van Hoevenberg for Mom at the 70-and-up rate.
  • A drive, not a flight day: about 10 hours 40 minutes from Virginia Beach with an overnight in the Hudson Valley on the way up, the way Patrick did it last time. Daniel flies to Albany.
  • The Mirror Lake Inn spa and a reformer studio on Main Street for Sara, the Wild Center and the skating oval for the kids (Taylor skates free), a hot tub for Mom.
  • About $28,000 to $36,000 for ten with the one house confirmed open.

The catch

  • The bobsled, Kristina's stated reason to come back, is not bookable: the venue's page redirects and the tourism site says it is unavailable for track updates. The skeleton ride that is offered is ages 13 and up, so only the adults could slide.
  • The walkable six-bedroom houses with hot tubs (Hillcrest Haven, the Stevens house, the Waterfront house) all show booked for December 27 to January 3. The one house confirmed open, Cliff View Loj, is under a mile out, which one reviewer called a 30-minute walk. Camp Kobl Hill, which would decide it, needs a call.
  • Cold: highs near 28 degrees, nights near zero.
  • Ten to eleven hours of driving each way, with winter on I-87 and Route 73 able to add hours.
  • No dedicated gluten-free restaurant in town; a list of celiac-literate kitchens and the house carry Sara's week. The toboggan chute needs a foot of lake ice, unlikely before January.

Weather that week. Reliably wintry and short-day: December highs about 28 degrees, nights near zero, about 104 inches of snow a year. Whiteface runs on snowmaking across nearly all of its skiable acres.

Stowe, Vermont, compared

Stowe has a house with a hot tub, a real spa at Topnotch or the Trapp Family Lodge, an Epic mountain for all five riders, and the groomed recreation path from the village. The drive is 12 to 13 hours from Virginia Beach, one to two more than Lake Placid; the village is small, so the house has to sit in it to be walkable; gluten-free depth is thin, no reformer studio was found, and there is no bobsled. Roughly $26,000 to $42,000 for ten, with two six-bedroom hot-tub houses found and neither priced for the week.

Mont-Tremblant

The pedestrian village, Quebec

Pedestrian village colorful roofs in snow with ski runs behind. Photo: Joe Shlabotnik from Forest Hills, Queens, USA, CC BY 2.0 (source)

A car-free village at the base of the mountain, lit up at night, with the skating rink, the Aquaclub water park, the open-air Cabriolet gondola and two dozen restaurants on foot. The mountain is low (about 750 to 2,900 feet) and on the Ikon pass. One six-bedroom house with a private hot tub is open and priced for the exact week. Getting there is a one-connection flight to Montreal for every household, then 90 minutes to two hours of winter driving, a border, and passports for all ten including the kids. Nights run near 5 degrees.

Why it works

  • A car-free village: heated walkways, a free lit skating rink with ice slides and free skate loans, the Cabriolet, sleigh rides from the village, the Brind'O Aquaclub for a cold afternoon.
  • Low altitude for Sara (base about 750 feet) and the Scandinave spa ten minutes away: an outdoor thermal circuit with silence as the house rule.
  • Real adventure beside the skiing: dog sledding seven minutes away where the kids ride with the guide from age 3, guided snowmobiles, an ice-climbing intro, 80 kilometers of Nordic trails at Domaine Saint-Bernard.
  • Tremblant's own advice for the holiday week: start on the North Side and ride the upper lifts to skip the 10 AM South Side line; January 1 is usually lighter.
  • Panache 784 is open and priced for December 27 to January 3: six bedrooms, a private outdoor hot tub, about $27,900 to $31,000 for the week, fees included.

The catch

  • Every household connects once to Montreal, then drives 90 minutes to two hours north in winter, likely after dark on arrival day, with a border each way.
  • Passports for all ten, including Bennett and Taylor. Child books are in-person with both parents; routine processing runs four to twelve weeks and the trip is about nine weeks out, so anyone short applies now and expedites.
  • The house is a shuttle ride from the village, not a walk: the listing says shuttle or a short drive, one guest calls it a 25-minute uphill hike, another a five-minute walk from the shuttle stop. The family accepts the free resort shuttle as the way home, or this option is out.
  • Nights near 5 degrees and highs in the 20s; wind can close the summit lifts.
  • No hospital in Mont-Tremblant; the nearest 24-hour ER is about 40 minutes away in Sainte-Agathe. Carry epinephrine from home. No dedicated gluten-free restaurant; the house kitchen plus celiac-literate places in town and the village.
  • About $45,000 to $66,000 for ten at the current exchange rate.

Weather that week. Real winter: December highs about 27 degrees, lows about 14; January highs about 19, lows about 5. Snow likely. Wind can close the summit lifts and leave the lower half open.

Money, in two currencies

Prices here are in US dollars at 1.42 Canadian dollars to the US dollar, the mid-October rate; the rate moved from 1.40 to 1.42 over the previous month, so Canadian-priced lines can shift a few percent either way. Vrbo quotes in US dollars; Airbnb and the operators quote in Canadian dollars with about 18 percent of tax and resort royalty on activities.

Not in any line: the Kidz Club and Snow School, which do not publish prices and fill months ahead for this week.
